# Void-Walker

Void-Walker is the walk home after the last starship left: near-black violet,
three layers of seeded starlight that drift with the camera, and cells that
flare white-violet at birth then settle to a cool purple glow as they age.
Deaths puff into a short, pooled particle fade. Chrome is translucent dark
glass with light bleeding at the edges and wide-tracked type that blooms out
of darkness — nothing snaps. Sound is bell-like plucks through a runtime-
synthesised convolution tail, over a deep evolving pad. There are no audio
files and no image files; the impulse and the starfield are both generated.
The strongest bloom of the six lives here, always under the L4 overlay.
