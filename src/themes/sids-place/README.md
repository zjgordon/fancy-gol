# Sids-Place

Sids-Place is a campaign map that has been unfolded too many times: warm
parchment, iron-gall ink, and cells that sit on the page as slightly irregular
hand-inked tiles. Wear comes with age — fresh marks are wet and dark, old ones
fade into the fibre. Multi-state rules read as terrain; this is the natural
home of Highlands/Liquid from the inception document, where water and highland
are pigments, not neon. Chrome is an illuminated manuscript: serif titles, a
modern sans for the numbers, vellum tabs, a settle in every motion as if the
panels had mass. Sound is paper, wood, and a low woodwind that never hurries.
